Telemetry Guide

🎯 Analog Needle (Suwi): The physical red needle automatically rotates from 0 to 160 based on your live vehicle speed.

🛰️ Hardware GPS: This app uses your phone or device's built-in high-accuracy satellite location sensor.

🚨 Over-Speed Siren: Set your speed threshold (e.g., 60 km/h). If you cross it, dual-frequency siren audio and red flashing visuals will engage.

🔄 Units: Switch anytime between KM/H and MPH with automatic needle scaling.
